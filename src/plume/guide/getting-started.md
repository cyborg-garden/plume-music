# Getting started

Plume plays a band with you. You play a real instrument, and plume listens, keeps your
tempo and comes in underneath you.

## Your first take

1. Press **Start**. Nothing plays yet.
2. Plume says "Play something." Play for a few seconds. It finds the channel you are on
   and checks your level. If the level is too hot or too quiet, it says what to turn.
3. It asks "Headphones on?" Say no if the band will come out of speakers.
4. With headphones on, it asks "Can you hear yourself?" Your answers decide whether plume
   plays your instrument back to you. That is Hear me, in [Settings](#/guide/sound).
5. Knock or clap 8 even beats. On a plugged-in guitar, play them on muted strings, since a
   knock on the body may not reach the pickup. The drums land where the ninth beat would be.

Now play. The drums stay alone until you do. The bass and chords come in about eight bars
after you start, and the melody four bars after that.

Next time, Start goes straight to the count. **Set up again** in Settings asks the
questions again.

## Ending a take

Press **Stop** at any time. On a plugged-in instrument you can also just stop playing:
the band fades out and the take ends by itself, about 20 seconds later, a little longer
at slow tempos.
A room mic hears the band as well as you, so it may never hear you stop. Press Stop there.

Stop before the band has come in and no take is kept, so a false start leaves nothing
behind.

## Listening back

When a take ends, the page opens its Record: a strip of what plume did and when, one
sentence for each moment, and a short roast. Press **Play** to listen. The buttons beside
it choose what you hear:

- **Together** is you and the band, lined up. Only takes from a plugged-in instrument have it.
- **Your input** or **Your mic** is just you.
- **The band** is just plume.

**Keep** marks a take as one to hold on to, and you can give it a name. Keep deletes
nothing. **Takes** in the header shows every take as a carved stone.

Each take gets its own new folder, under `~/Music/Plume` unless `plume gui --takes DIR`
names another. Settings shows where they go.
