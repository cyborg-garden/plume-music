# Bundled samples

`kit/` is a small selection from Virtuosity Drums by Versilian Studios
(https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums), released under CC0-1.0.
Each hit blends the mid, overhead and room mics of one take, then is trimmed,
aligned to 1 ms before the attack, and normalised.

`tuned/` holds pitched one-shots, trimmed and normalised:

- `fingerbass_*`: Sneakybass by Karoryfer Samples, CC0-1.0
  (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.sneakybass).
- `vibes_*`: Vibraphone from the Versilian Community Sample Library, CC0-1.0
  (https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL).
- `wurli_*`: Wurlitzer EP200 by Greg Sullivan, CC-BY-3.0
  (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos).
  Attribution required: "Wurlitzer EP200 Electric Piano" samples by Greg Sullivan,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/, trimmed and normalised for plume.
